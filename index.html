I'm a first-year CS student at Georgia Tech on the Cybersecurity & Privacy and Artificial Intelligence threads. Most of what I make sits where those two meet — agentic systems, LLM tooling, automation that removes real hours of work.

Away from the terminal I film and edit Polestar, a vlog series I produce independently — 105 episodes so far.
